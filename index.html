📍 Directions to your camp

🧭 Get directions (Google Maps)

How to get there

  1. Tap Get directions. Google Maps starts from your current location and guides you step by step.
  2. Want to see where the camp is first? Tap Show camp on map here.
  3. In the state capital, ask for the "NYSC Orientation Camp". Bike and taxi drivers know it, especially during camping season.
  4. Agree on the fare before you enter a bike or cab.
  5. Save the map to offline use in Google Maps if your network is weak.
Camp locations can change. Check your call-up letter or the official NYSC portal for your exact camp.

Public groups are open to strangers. Never send money, documents or your home address to anyone in a group, and be careful who you travel with.

Travel safety tips

  • Travel in daylight. Leave early so you reach camp before dark.
  • Board only at a busy public park. Never meet at a private or isolated place.
  • Never accept a ride in a private car offered by a stranger.
  • Don't pay the full fare to an unknown individual before you travel.
  • Only post transport you have arranged yourself. This site does not list drivers or vouch for anyone.
  • Share your live location on WhatsApp for the whole trip.
  • Keep your call-up letter and money in different bags. Keep valuables close.

Registration steps (general)

  1. Arrive at camp with your printed call-up letter and original documents.
  2. Go through document screening at the registration hall.
  3. Complete the medical checks and biometrics.
  4. Collect your kit and accommodation slot, and get your platoon.

🛑 Before you pay for a house

  • Inspect in person, in daylight, with someone. Never pay for a place you have not seen.
  • Don't send money to someone you haven't met. Fake landlords and agents target new corpers.
  • Be careful with rushed payments, "many people are interested" and rent that is too cheap.
  • Ask for a receipt or written agreement, and confirm the person really owns or manages the place.
  • Meet a possible roommate in a public place first. Send the address and their number to someone you trust.
  • Ask other corpers in your state group which areas are safe.
